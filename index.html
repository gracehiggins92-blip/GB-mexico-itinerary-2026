Isla Holbox · slow island days

6 nights · 5–11 NovStay: Punta Caliza

Your Holbox base is Punta Caliza. The plan is deliberately relaxed: enjoy the hotel and pool, spend time by the beach, wander into town, and mix casual seafood/tacos with one or two special meals.

  • 5 Nov: Arrive, settle in, and keep the evening easy.
  • 6 Nov: Pool and beach day; no fixed plans.
  • 7 Nov: Explore town at a gentle pace; optional special dinner at Milpa (suggestion only, not booked).
  • 8 Nov: Open beach/pool day; casual seafood meal if you fancy it.
  • 9 Nov: Another slow day for swimming, reading and a long lunch.
  • 10 Nov: Final full day; revisit a favourite spot and enjoy a nice dinner.
  • 11 Nov: Travel day to Oaxaca via Chiquilá and Cancún; ferry and transfer still to be arranged.
